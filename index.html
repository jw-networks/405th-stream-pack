Remote Director

Switch the show from any browser: preview, TAKE, camera multiview, team views, custom 2/3/4-ups and auto cycle.

Open the Director   Audio mixer

When it opens, click the red Offline badge and enter:

  1. Address: the show's secure tunnel address, e.g. wss://something.trycloudflare.com (ask the show runner).
  2. Password: the OBS WebSocket password (ask the show runner).

Names and camera previews load automatically from the show once you're connected.
